  • Drag — rotate
  • Scroll — zoom
  • Double-click the Sun, Earth or Moon — fly to it and follow
  • Click Earth — sky view from that spot
  • Hold on Earth — real-time speed
  • Parameters panel — time, distance, Now, Where am I
  • Just a page made for fun. No location data is collected or stored: your position stays in your browser, and only the selected point's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get its weather.
