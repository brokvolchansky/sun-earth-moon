  • Drag — rotate
  • Scroll — zoom
  • Double-click the Sun, Earth or Moon — fly to it and follow
  • Click Earth — sky view from that spot
  • Hold on Earth — real-time speed
  • 📍 Where am I — the sky above you
  • Parameters panel — time, distance, Now, quality
  • Just a page made for fun. Anonymous page-view statistics are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, no personal profiles). No location data is collected or stored: your position stays in your browser, and only the selected point's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get its weather (clicking a constellation asks Wikipedia for its description — just the name).
